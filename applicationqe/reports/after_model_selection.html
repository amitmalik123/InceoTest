    Important Information about the Risks of Investing in Private Assets

    Private asset investments are generally only appropriate for experienced investors who understand and can bear the associated risks, including the risk of complete loss.

    These investments are typically illiquid and may not be sold or accessed for extended periods of time. Because of this limited liquidity, we recommend keeping initial exposure to no more than 15% of the total portfolio.

    Clients with short-term liquidity needs or limited time horizons should carefully assess whether private assets fit their financial goals.

    Risks considerations include:

    • Lack of liquidity: Redemption of fund shares is usually only available on a periodic (not daily) and limited basis.
    • Limited ability to update your portfolio: Private assets reduce the ability to adjust your portfolio allocations, which can cause deviations from your asset allocation investment plans and affect account performance.
    • Valuation of Fund Shares: Private assets are difficult to value, and when redeemed, you may not receive the same value as shown on your account statements.
    • SBLOC: Allocating to private assets will typically impact the advance rates and line of credit size if used in a Securities-Backed Line of Credit pledged account.
